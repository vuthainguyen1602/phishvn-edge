\section{Limitations and future work}
The initial release includes the export and CPU benchmark path, a synthetic model generator, and historical aggregate snapshots. The original trained URL detectors, private test splits, and raw device logs are not bundled. Consequently, the public repository reproduces the software demonstration and aggregate visualisation, but not the original detector evaluation or hardware campaign.

TensorRT engine conversion and FP16 classification validation remain outside this release. Longer thermal soaks, external power-meter checks, neural text models, and INT8 calibration are also pending. Synthetic numeric inputs test inference execution rather than the distribution of real messages or URLs. A complete deployment study would additionally measure feature extraction, request handling, and service behaviour under realistic traffic.
